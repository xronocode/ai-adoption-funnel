## Consensus

All seven agreed on the **order of magnitude** of every layer. Disagreements were about precision (Layer 4 central value, Layer 6 interval width) and framing (share-first vs count-first), not about the funnel's shape.

## Deferred concepts (future work)

Raised in review, not yet built — candidates for a v2.0:

1. **Leverage density layer** — economic displacement, not usage intensity (10 orchestrators may outweigh 1,000 casual users)
2. **Institutional friction coefficients** — per-sector modifiers (approval latency, data fragmentation, procurement)
3. **7-stage maturity segmentation** — awareness → experimentation → habitual → embedding → orchestration → institutional → AI-native
4. **Temporal confidence decay** — formal freshness weighting as metrics age
5. **Formal stratification metric** — a Gini-style coefficient of AI productivity
